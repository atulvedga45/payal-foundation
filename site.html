About Our Trust

Registered Social Welfare NGO in Mumbai

Established under the Public Trust Act (Reg No: G.B.B.S.D 409/2026 | F-82401 (M)), Young Samaj Seva Trust operates under the leadership of President Zakir Hussain and Secretary Mohd Rahim Abdul Kalam Khan.

Our core objective is to organize grassroots community initiatives, empower youth through skill enhancement, and extend support to underserved populations in the Greater Mumbai region.
